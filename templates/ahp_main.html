📋 Tips for Accurate Comparisons:
  • Consistency matters: If A is 3x more important than B, and B is 5x more important than C, then A should be about 15x more important than C.
  • Default value is 1: If two hazards are equally important, leave the value at 1.
  • Reciprocal handling: The system automatically calculates reverse comparisons.
  • Consistency check: You'll see a Consistency Ratio (CR) in results. CR < 0.1 is good.

C - Machinery Hazards: Amputation, entanglement

D - Chemical Hazards: Toxic exposure, burns

E - Environmental Hazards: Heat stress, cold exposure

F - Fire & Explosion: Burns, structural damage

G - Ergonomic Hazards: Back injury, strain

H - Biological Hazards: Infections, disease

I - Organizational Hazards: Poor communication, fatigue
